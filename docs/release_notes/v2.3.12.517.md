CueMol 2.3.12 (build 517)

This release is about making what is already there hold up: an audit of the core
library fixed 132 latent bugs, and a rebuild of CueMol3's internals brought several
dozen real fixes with it, including two ways of losing unsaved work. One new
setting: the border caps on a density-map surface are now yours to control.

CueMol2 (the UXP app) is unchanged apart from the shared core, which is where most
of this release's fixes live. Note that CueMol3 is still a work in progress.

> **Intel/AMD users:** the x86-64 builds continue to require a CPU with **AVX2**
> (Intel Haswell / AMD Zen or newer, roughly 2013 and later), as they have since
> 2.3.10.504.

---

## Core library: 132 fixes from a full audit

The shared C++ core was audited end to end; 132 of 134 findings are fixed here, and
both apps benefit. It covers memory-safety bugs that surfaced as unexplained crashes,
silently wrong results in atom-name matching, the proline donor, a PDB file's default
model, undo/redo and BRIX map statistics, leaks of labels, shaders, streams and
tables, and validation of malformed surface, CCP4/MTZ and XTC files as well as of
script arguments. Damaged `HELIX`/`SHEET` records and mismatched trajectory blocks
are now skipped with a warning rather than failing the whole load, and POV-Ray export
writes its label images beside the `.pov` file. All 1523 tests pass.

## Density maps and surfaces

**The border cap on a map surface is its own setting.** The faces that close an
isosurface at the edge of the displayed region used to be decided entirely by the
region mode; they are now a **Cap mode** property. *Auto* reproduces what existing
scenes look like, and *On* caps even in box mode — the useful one for figures, since
otherwise you see through the open edge onto the surface's back face. Caps are also
clipped at the molecule boundary now, where before they could stick out past a
clipped surface or spike back to the grid origin.

## Opening files

**Large GROMACS `.gro` files load.** Above 99999 residues the residue number rolls
over to zero, and CueMol read that as the same residue: the first solvent residue
merged into the protein's residue 1 and the read aborted with `Failed to append
atom`, so nothing loaded at all. Rolled-over residues are now renumbered, and
duplicated atoms are skipped with a warning.

## CueMol3: crashes and lost work

- **Pasting a molecule into a new scene no longer takes the app down** — one copied
  with its preset renderer group carried a group whose name was never written out.
- **Deleting an animation element that others are timed against no longer bricks the
  animation panel**; the dependants keep the position they sat at.
- **Two paths that could discard an unsaved scene are closed**: a 10-second watchdog
  on close/quit, and a second instance wiping the first's working directories.
- **File > Reload Scene no longer duplicates the whole scene**, and a scene that
  fails to open says so instead of leaving an empty tab and a console message.
- **A recoverable error no longer ends the session**, a damaged application-state
  file no longer leaves the app windowless, the menus no longer lock up after a
  reload, and Redo survives an empty undo transaction.

## CueMol3: working in the app

**The Inspector's Properties pages were rebuilt** — all 20 renderer pages plus the
common, object, group and Scene pages now come from one description, so they behave
alike. Numeric drag fields cover their whole range (dragging used to need 400–800 px
of travel), tesselation *detail* is a dropdown, the atom-count badge finally shows on
Selection rows, and the `disorder` renderer is back in New Renderer and now draws.

**Dragging a number behaves**: Shift is a real precision mode instead of jumping
about six steps per pixel, and Esc always cancels rather than quietly committing the
value. **Colouring modes are no longer one-way** — a direct-surface, `dsurf2` or
contour renderer switched to electrostatic-potential or multi-gradient colouring
could not be switched back.

**Lists respond to the keyboard**, **Cut/Copy/Paste and Undo/Redo** are real commands
that follow the focus, a copied renderer can be pasted onto another renderer at last,
and **all 32 dialogs share one frame**, so they agree on width, padding, buttons and
theming.

**The UI keeps up.** Changing the perspective or the centre mark fired no event at
all, so the View menu and Inspector went stale, and switching tabs could leave the
previous tab's scene tree on screen with Undo/Redo stuck disabled. Startup is no
longer a slideshow either: windows stay hidden until painted, and the oversized
centre mark that flashed on the first frame is gone.

**Interaction is cheaper.** Input events reach the worker without waiting for a
reply — there was one per event per frame — and payloads are parsed only when read,
which matters when loading a scene fires hundreds of them.

## Under the hood

Much of this release is internal restructuring with no behaviour change: the worker's
service contract, the renderer source tree, the shared component library's boundary
and the Electron main process were split into enforced units with tests.

## Known incompatibilities

- **Skipped duplicate atoms in a `.gro` file** make the atom count differ from the
  file, so an attached `xtc`/`trr`/`dcd` trajectory will not line up. Renumbered
  residues carry a +100000 offset (original: `index % 100000`).
- **`disorder` and `atomintr` are no longer offered in File Open** — they draw
  nothing right after a load. Both remain in New Renderer.
- **An empty or degenerate density map** leaves the Inspector blank: its properties
  contain infinities and fail to parse. Nothing crashes, and real data avoids it.
- **Clipboard payloads copied before this release** paste a renderer group with no
  name; copy again from a live scene.
- **Cancelling Get PDB** no longer reports a `canceled` field, and error dialogs drop
  the `Error: ` prefix.
- **Surface-object generation does not clip at the molecule boundary**, even with a
  boundary molecule set; only the display path does.
- **The Linux X11 backend is excluded from the build** by default; restore it with
  `ENABLE_X11_SYSDEP`.
- **Smaller ones:** the Error alert dialog lost its window-frame close button, a time
  field's arrow keys step a digit only on the first press, and the never-triggerable
  application-wide zoom level was removed.
