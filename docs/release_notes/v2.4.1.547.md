CueMol 2.4.1 (build 547)

This release is about **speed and large structures**. In CueMol3, MD trajectory
and morph playback of **tube, ribbon, cartoon and nucleic-acid** renderers is
several times faster, and on **Windows** much faster still. Building a
**cartoon** of a large structure takes seconds instead of half a minute (4V6X:
32 s -> 1 s), surface coloring is faster, and several crashes on very large
structures (4V6X, 3J3Q) are fixed.

The Windows builds of both apps are now compiled with clang-cl, and
`tritium_cli` can be installed from npm.

---

## Faster playback of tube / ribbon / cartoon / nucleic acid (CueMol3)

- These renderers now keep their mesh on the GPU. When atoms move (MD
  trajectory, morphing) or the coloring changes, the mesh is rewritten in place
  instead of being rebuilt, and the work is split across CPU cores.
- On 4V6X (human 80S ribosome), the per-frame update of a ribbon went from
  about 120 ms to about 12 ms, and of a cartoon from about 90 ms to 26 ms.
  Small molecules no longer wake the worker threads, which saves CPU.
- Tube and ribbon share vertices between neighboring rings, so much less data
  is sent to the GPU every frame.
- **Windows**: updated vertex buffers are now uploaded the way Direct3D 11
  expects, and normals use a format it supports natively. 4V6X ribbon morphing
  went from 8 to 46 updates per second (RTX 4070).
- The nucleic-acid renderer (`nucl`) now follows morphs and repaints in place,
  in every mode.
- The image is unchanged: in-place updates were checked to be pixel-identical
  to a full rebuild.

## Faster cartoon build (CueMol3 and CueMol2)

- The cartoon's smoothing splines are computed with a new linear-time
  algorithm. `show cartoon` on 4V6X takes about 1 s instead of 32 s.
- The result is pixel-identical to the previous version.

## Surfaces (CueMol3 and CueMol2)

- **Faster coloring by atom** for molecular surfaces (molsurf), direct surfaces
  (dsurface) and map isosurfaces: up to about 5x faster on large surfaces.
- **dsurface ignores alternate conformations** (only atoms with no alternate
  location or location `A` are used, as molsurf already did). Structures with
  alternate conformations build faster and without extra fragments.
- molsurf no longer keeps a large cache for the whole life of the surface
  (about 700 MB on 4V6X).

## Fixes

- Molecular surfaces (molsurf, and dsurface with the MeshMS algorithm) no
  longer crash on 4V6X.
- A cartoon too large to draw (e.g. HIV-1 capsid 3J3Q, 2.4 M atoms) no longer
  stops CueMol3: it is left undrawn and a message is logged. The 3J3Q ribbon
  and tube are drawn.
- Tube, ribbon and nucleic-acid renderers no longer log `coeff not found` for
  very short chain segments (every frame during playback).

## Windows build (CueMol3 and CueMol2)

- Both apps are now built with clang-cl instead of MSVC. Rendering with the
  ray tracer (umbreon) is about 6-8% faster; images are unchanged.

## `tritium_cli` on npm

- `npm install -g @cuemol/tritium-cli` installs the console CLI on its own.
  It finds an installed CueMol3 (or `CUEMOL3_APP`) and starts it; without one,
  it prints the download URL.
- The interactive banner shows the app icon, and the CLI warns when its
  protocol version differs from the running app's.

## Known incompatibilities and limitations

- A tube / ribbon / cartoon whose mesh would exceed about 2 GB is not drawn
  (it used to crash). Drawing it at lower detail is not implemented yet.
- dsurface now leaves out a residue that has only `B` / `C` conformers and no
  `A` (as molsurf does).
- molsurf now starts the molecule's own coloring scheme, as other renderers
  do. With a stateful scheme (e.g. rainbow) set on the molecule, molsurf colors
  may change to match the other renderers.
- Regenerating a molsurf with only a new density takes longer on very large
  structures (4V6X: 1.5 s -> 2.8 s), since the cache is gone.
- Ball & stick ring plates are no longer picked (a plate stands for no single
  atom).
- `tritium_cli` from npm has been tested against an installed app on macOS
  only.
